Nanoscale infrared imaging

AFM-IR and s-SNOM instrumentation at Stony Brook University

Working with Profs. Xiaoji Xu and Mengkun Liu, I helped align and calibrate nanoscale infrared imaging instrumentation (AFM-IR and s-SNOM) and built two tools around the measurements.

Where
Stony Brook University
With
Profs. Xiaoji Xu and Mengkun Liu
Period
2025–2026
Tools
AFM-IR, s-SNOM, Moku:Go, Python

What I did

  • Alignment and calibration. I helped align and calibrate the nanoscale infrared imaging instrumentation.
  • Triggered FFT on Moku:Go. I implemented a hardware-triggered, 1,024-point FFT on Moku:Go.
  • Topo2stl. I developed a Python tool that converts measured surface topography into printable 3D models.
