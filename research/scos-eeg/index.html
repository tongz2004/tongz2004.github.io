Time-gated SCOS and EEG

Optical acquisition and hardware synchronization at Stony Brook University

In Prof. Ulas Sunar’s group I work on the acquisition side of a time-gated speckle contrast optical spectroscopy (SCOS) system: the control software that synchronizes the hardware, the routines that check and process the data, and an acquisition workflow that combines SCOS with EEG.

Where
Stony Brook University
Group
Prof. Ulas Sunar
Period
2026–present
Tools
Python, NI-DAQ, MATLAB, C-RED 2 camera, galvanometer, EEG

What I do

  • Hardware synchronization. I develop Python-based NI-DAQ control that synchronizes the camera with the galvanometer.
  • Automated alignment. The same NI-DAQ control code supports automated alignment.
  • Data quality control and processing. I write MATLAB routines for data quality control and processing.
  • Acquisition debugging. I investigated residual frames and mismatched frame tags in high-speed acquisition with a C-RED 2 camera.
  • SCOS with EEG. I am building and debugging acquisition that combines SCOS and EEG. This part is in progress.
